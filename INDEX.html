The Story

One writer, one voice, one digital muse.

"I'm a songwriter who created an AI artist so the world could finally hear the songs living in my head."

Every lyric and melody starts with me. I've been using my AI artist Alisha Inari to amplify those songs and bring them to life — she's the face that carries the music into the world while I keep writing.

My goal is to bring these songs to life in a whole new way — my own voice, in a real studio. That's the version still waiting to be born, and it's where you come in.

Support the human behind the music

There's nothing wrong with an AI artist — Alisha is a gift. She carries these songs to people and places I could never reach on my own, and I'm proud of everything we create together. But every word starts with a real, human heart, and my dream is to step up to the mic and record these songs the way only a person can. That's what your support makes possible: backing the human side of this art and helping the real version come to life. If the music moves you, being part of that journey would mean the world.
