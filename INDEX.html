Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might be wondering before we make your song.

Do you use AI to make the songs?+

Yes — and I’m completely open about it. I write everything myself — the lyrics, melody, and structure — then bring the song to life using AI vocals — mainly through my AI artist, Alisha Inari, and a male vocalist can be arranged if you prefer. If you’d rather have a real human voice, I also offer live vocals sung by me as an upgrade.

What’s the difference between AI and human vocals?+

AI vocals are mainly my AI artist Alisha Inari (a male vocalist can be arranged too), and are faster and more affordable. Human vocals are sung live by me and include studio time, mixing & mastering — a premium option that takes a little longer. Both include the same original songwriting and production.

How long does it take?+

AI-vocal songs: about 5–10 business days. Human-vocal songs: 2–4 weeks, since they include studio recording and mixing. Need it sooner? Rush delivery is available for +25%.

How many revisions do I get?+

Birthday songs include 1 revision, and all other custom songs include up to 2 rounds of revisions — so we get it just right. Additional tweaks are $50 each.

Can I use my song commercially?+

Personal songs (like birthdays) are yours to enjoy and share. For business or commercial use — ads, brand campaigns, broadcast, or anything monetized — we’ll set up the right license, quoted to your project. You get full usage rights for the agreed purpose; I keep the songwriting & publishing unless we arrange a separate buyout.

What files will I receive?+

High-quality WAV + MP3 files, ready to use. Stems (separate track-outs) are available as an add-on.

How does payment work?+

For custom projects, a 50% deposit books your spot and the remaining 50% is due on delivery, paid by secure invoice. Smaller flat-rate songs are paid upfront. Deposits are non-refundable once work begins, but I’ll always use your included revisions to make sure you’re happy.

What can you write a song about?+

Almost anything — weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, love, milestones, empowerment, your brand, a podcast intro, you name it. Share your story, vibe, and any references, and I’ll shape it into something that sounds like you.

Do you create explicit content?+

No — I keep my music clean and welcoming. I don’t write, record, or produce songs with profanity or sexual content, so every song I make is something you can share and play anywhere, for any audience.
