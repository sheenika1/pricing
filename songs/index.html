Questions

Good to know before you order

How do I pay?

After you send your request, I'll email you within 24 hours to confirm the details and send your payment link. Writing begins once payment is received.

Who sings the song?

I write every lyric myself. The music and vocals are produced with AI tools, which lets me deliver a fully finished song in days instead of weeks.

What if I want something changed?

The Custom Song and Rush Song include one round of changes. Tell me what to adjust and I'll update it.

Can I share it online or play it at my event?

Yes. Your song is yours to play at your wedding, share with family, and post on your personal social media.

How much should I tell you?

The more real details you share, the more personal the song. Names, nicknames, where you met, a funny moment, a hard season you got through together. Those details make a song feel like it could only be about them.
